# Exploratory Data Analysis Figures (`reports/figures/eda/`)

High-resolution 300 DPI publication diagnostic figures generated by the `AdvancedEDAPipeline` (`scripts/run_advanced_eda.py`).

## Catalog of Figures
1. `eda_photometric_distributions.png`: Global RGB and HSV color channel histograms and kernel density estimations across all 2,522 dataset images.
2. `eda_foreground_chromaticity.png`: Foreground chromaticity (Hue vs Saturation) centroid clusters for each denomination, isolating banknote pixels from ambient backgrounds.
3. `eda_spatial_geometry.png`: Bounding box aspect ratios, width/height normalized distributions, and area coverage statistics.
4. `eda_spatial_heatmap.png`: 2D spatial density accumulation heatmap of bounding box centroids revealing lens framing center biases.
5. `eda_sharpness_frequency.png`: Laplacian variance distribution quantifying motion blur and optical defocus across capturing environments.
6. `eda_boundary_truncation.png`: Analysis of edge clipping and boundary truncation when banknotes intersect image borders.
7. `eda_deep_dive_confusions.png`: High-resolution visual examples of denomination pairs with high visual similarity (e.g. 10 AZN teal vs 20 AZN green).
8. `near_duplicate_pair_example.png`: Visual confirmation of near-duplicate detection and pruning via perceptual hashing (pHash) and DINOv2 latent cosine similarity.
9. `dataset_sample_batch_grid.png`: Comprehensive numismatic grid illustrating sample banknote captures across diverse lighting, backgrounds, and angles.
